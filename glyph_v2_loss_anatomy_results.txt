INTERPRETATION GUIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lower overall loss/PPL means better next-character prediction on held-out validation text.
2. Space recall/F1 directly tests whether the model predicts word boundaries when the next character is a space.
3. If 100k has lower overall loss but worse space recall, some of its loss improvement is not translating into better word boundaries.
4. Compare letter vs whitespace vs punctuation loss to see which character classes improved most with training.
5. This benchmark is diagnostic; it does not produce an overall model ranking.
6. The stored best-validation loss is from the training script's sampled validation checks; 'Full validation loss' here is recomputed deterministically across the entire held-out split.
